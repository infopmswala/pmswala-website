Our Core Business

Bank seized property and farmland, handled end to end

Two asset classes we work in directly, not just advise on: distressed bank-seized property that we buy and sell ourselves, and farmland positioned as a long-term hold. This is the foundation PMSWALA was built on.

Property

We buy and sell bank-seized properties

Beyond advisory, PMSWALA directly transacts in bank-seized residential, commercial, and industrial property. We shortlist opportunities, run legal and title validation, and either broker the sale to a vetted buyer or acquire the asset ourselves for resale.

  • Active buy-side sourcing across bank and NBFC auction pipelines
  • Sell-side representation for investors exiting a distressed asset
  • Full title, encumbrance, and possession checks before any transaction
Farmland

Farmland as a long-term investment

We also identify and structure farmland investments for clients who want exposure to agricultural land: a lower-volatility asset class with steady long-term appreciation and, in many cases, income from cultivation or lease.

  • Land-title and irrigation-access verification before purchase
  • Soil, water table, and regional crop-yield assessment
  • Lease and cultivation-partner arrangements for hands-off ownership
