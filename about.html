About PMSWALA

From advisory roots to EV-tech execution leadership

We combine startup speed with institutional clarity. The mission is simple: help clients make sharper infrastructure and capital decisions with confidence, transparency, and long-term value.

Our Story

Built by people who screened deals before we ever built software

PMSWALA did not start as a platform. It started as an investment advisory practice, screening opportunities for a small group of high-conviction clients who wanted a second, sharper set of eyes before committing capital.

As those clients began asking about distressed bank-seized assets and, later, EV charging infrastructure, the advisory work grew into something that needed a system behind it, not just a spreadsheet and a phone call. That system is now PMSWALA: an execution-led platform where advisory judgment and live data work together.

Today the same discipline that shaped our first client screenings runs underneath every feature on the platform, from the Compliance and Risk Layer to the Investor Control Room.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision, and the standard we hold ourselves to

Mission

Help founders, operators, and institutions make sharper EV infrastructure and capital decisions, with confidence, transparency, and long-term value at the center of every recommendation.

Vision

A next-generation energy economy where every charging asset and every distressed-asset opportunity is backed by investor-grade intelligence before a decision is made, not after.

Standard

Startup speed, institutional discipline. Every engagement is judged against one question: would this decision hold up under a serious investor's due diligence?
