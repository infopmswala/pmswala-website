About PMSWALA

From advisory roots to real estate execution leadership

We combine startup speed with institutional clarity. The mission is simple: help clients make sharper property, farmland, and capital decisions with confidence, transparency, and long-term value.

Our Story

Built by people who screened deals before we ever built software

PMSWALA did not start as a platform. It started as an investment advisory practice, screening opportunities for a small group of high-conviction clients who wanted a second, sharper set of eyes before committing capital.

As those clients began asking about distressed bank-seized property and farmland, the advisory work grew into direct buying and selling — PMSWALA started transacting itself, not just advising. In 2024, we added a new, fast-growing chapter: EV charging infrastructure advisory. That system is now PMSWALA: an execution-led platform where advisory judgment and live data work together.

Today the same discipline that shaped our first client screenings runs underneath every part of the platform, from Compliance and Title Verification to the Investor Control Room.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision, and the standard we hold ourselves to

Mission

Help founders, families, and institutions make sharper property, farmland, and capital decisions, with confidence, transparency, and long-term value at the center of every recommendation.

Vision

A market where every bank-seized property, farmland parcel, and distressed-asset opportunity is backed by investor-grade intelligence before a decision is made, not after.

Standard

Startup speed, institutional discipline. Every engagement is judged against one question: would this decision hold up under a serious investor's due diligence?
